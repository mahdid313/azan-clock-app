# 📱 AZAN CLOCK - User Guide

## Turn Your Old Phone Into a Prayer Clock

---

## 📥 Installation (5 Minutes)

### Step 1: Download the App

Get the APK file (app installer) from your administrator.

### Step 2: Enable Installation

1. Open **Settings** on your Android phone
2. Go to **Security** or **Privacy**
3. Enable **Install from Unknown Sources** or **Allow from this source**

### Step 3: Install

1. Open your **File Manager** or **Downloads** folder
2. Tap the `azan-clock.apk` file
3. Tap **Install**
4. Wait for installation to complete
5. Tap **Open**

---

## ⚙️ First Time Setup

### Launching the App

1. Find **Azan Clock** in your app drawer
2. Tap to open
3. The app will open in fullscreen

### Initial Configuration

Tap the ⚙️ **Settings** icon (top left) to configure:

#### **Display Settings**
- Choose your color theme (Green, Blue, Purple)
- Toggle Light/Dark mode
- Adjust font size
- Select 12 or 24-hour format
- Show/hide seconds, date, sunrise, battery

#### **Volume Settings**
- **Azan Volume:** 0-100% (prayer call audio)
- **Duas Volume:** 0-100% (daily duas/recitations)
- **Tasbeeh Volume:** 0-100% (reminder chimes)

#### **Prayer Timing**
- Select your location from the dropdown
- Default: Hujjat Stanmore
- More locations will be added via updates

#### **Azan Audio**
Choose your preferred azan recording:
- Kadhmain
- Karbala
- Mashhad
- Najaf
- Qom

#### **Duas Scheduling**
Schedule daily duas:
- Select day of week
- Choose dua from list (26 available)
- Set fixed time (e.g., 08:00) OR relative to prayer (e.g., Fajr +30 min)

#### **Tasbeeh Reminders**
- Enable periodic tasbeeh chimes
- Set interval (15, 30, 60 minutes, etc.)
- Choose audio (Alhamdulillah, Salawat, Subhanallah, Takbeer)

### Save Settings

Tap **Close** when done - settings save automatically!

## 🕌 Using the Clock

### Main Screen

Your phone will display:
- **Current time** (large, center)
- **Current date** (top)
- **Prayer times** (below clock)
- **Countdown** to next prayer
- **Tomorrow's times** (after Maghrib)
- **Battery indicator** (optional)

### Automatic Features

The app runs 24/7 with:
- ✅ **Auto Azan:** Plays at Fajr, Zohr, and Maghrib
- ✅ **Scheduled Duas:** Plays at configured times
- ✅ **Tasbeeh Reminders:** Plays at set intervals
- ✅ **Screen Always On:** Never goes to sleep
- ✅ **Landscape Mode:** Always horizontal
- ✅ **Auto-Dim:** Dims after Maghrib to save battery

## 🔋 Battery Tips

### For 24/7 Use

1. Keep phone **plugged in** to charger
2. Enable **Auto-Dim** in settings (dims after Maghrib)
3. Lower **screen brightness** in settings
4. Disable **battery optimization** for this app:
   - Settings → Battery → Battery Optimization
   - Find "Azan Clock"
   - Select "Don't optimize"

### Battery Life

- **Plugged in:** Runs indefinitely
- **On battery:** ~8-16 hours (depends on brightness)

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### App Closes Automatically

**Solution:**
1. Settings → Apps → Azan Clock
2. Battery → Battery Optimization → Don't optimize
3. Background Restrictions → Unrestricted

### No Azan Playing

**Check:**
- Volume is not at 0%
- Phone is not on silent mode
- Tap mute button to unmute

### Wrong Prayer Times

**Solution:**
- Open settings and select correct location
- Wait for update (new timings added regularly)
- Or upload custom CSV

### Screen Turns Off

**Solution:**
- App needs permission to keep screen on
- Reinstall and grant permissions
- Disable battery optimization

### Audio Cuts Off

**Solution:**
- Another app might be interrupting
- Close other apps
- Ensure phone has enough storage

## 📤 Uploading Custom Timings

If your location isn't in the list:

1. Get a CSV file in this format:
```
Date,Fajr,Sunrise,Zohr,Maghrib
01/01/2025,06:15,08:06,12:08,16:03
```

2. Open **Settings** in the app
3. Tap **Upload CSV**
4. Select your file
5. It will appear in the timing dropdown
