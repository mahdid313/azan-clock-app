## How to Replace

1. Keep your original audio files organized in matching folders
2. Ensure filenames match exactly (case-sensitive!)
3. Recommended format: MP3, 128kbps
4. Copy your files to replace the placeholders
5. Rebuild APK: npm run build

---

## File Size Expectations

- With placeholders: ~8MB APK
- With real audio (128kbps): ~50-100MB APK
- With high quality (320kbps): ~100-150MB APK

Lower bitrate = smaller APK = faster downloads for users
Higher bitrate = better quality = larger APK

Recommended: 128kbps MP3 for good balance
